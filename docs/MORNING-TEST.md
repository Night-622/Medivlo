# Morning test: 20 minutes on tonight’s build

A guided first look at the live test site, **medivlo.web.app**. Each step says what to do, where, and what you
should see. Keys are the defaults; if you rebind them, the game’s hints follow your keys. If anything looks wrong,
note the step number.

Play a **Rimecaller** for this test. Its home city, Glacierhome, has the Runeforge you need in step 8.

---

**1. Make a hero (2 min)**
- On the title, choose **New Game** (↑/↓ and Enter, or click).
- In character creation, press ↓ until **Rimecaller** is picked, type a name, and press **Enter**.
- **You should see:** a short opening scene. Space moves the lines on. Esc skips the text but keeps what your choices
  did.

**2. First Steps (3 min)**
- The tracker on the right lists **First Steps**. Do each one: walk (**WASD**), dash (**Space**), attack (**1**),
  drink a draught (**Q**), open your Pack (**I**), pick a quest in the Journal (**J**) and track it (**T**), then walk
  up to the waystone and press **F** (“Wake”).
- **You should see:** each line ticks as you do it.

**3. Follow a quest marker (2 min)**
- Track **The First Tick** in the Journal (**J**, then **T**).
- Look at the minimap: a **gold diamond** marks where to go, or a gold arrow on its edge points the way.
- Press **M** for the map: the same place has a gold gem with its name beside it. In the Journal, **Show on map** opens
  the map on it.
- Walk to the lamplighter and press **F**, then talk to Halla Vint.
- **You should see:** the marker sits on the person or object, not the middle of a district. Things you have
  already looked at say “Already read”, “Already searched” or “Already seen” on their **F** prompt.

**4. The people around you (1 min)**
- Press **P**.
- **You should see:** the people of the realm you are in, not Brindlecross. Tab picks the filters (Everyone, Story,
  Trade, Villagers), the arrow keys move between people, and Esc closes.

**5. Loot and tooltips (2 min)**
- Fight a little (click an enemy, or use **1**–**3**). Open your Pack (**I**) and hover a piece of gear you picked up.
- **You should see:** the item name in its rarity colour; a line like “Sword · Fine · Item level 3”; extra bonus
  lines on Fine and better gear; the words around the name (like “of Vigour”) come from those lines; empty sockets
  are listed. Rarities run Common, Fine, Ember-touched, Runed, Wrought, Sparkforged.

**6. The skill tree and keystones (2 min)**
- When you level up, press **K** for Skills. The tabs **1**, **2**, **3** switch between **Spells**, **Skills** and
  **Upgrades**.
- Click a node to read it, and click again to learn it.
- **You should see:** ranked perks that tell you what the next rank does. The large nodes are **keystones**: one at the
  top of the Skills branch from your school, and shared keystones in Upgrades. You can hold two shared keystones.

**7. Spells and rune slots (1 min)**
- Press **B** for Spells.
- **You should see:** spell cards with rune slots. Slots open as you learn more of a spell’s path. You won’t have a
  rune yet: they turn up in lair chests later and can be crafted at the Runeforge.

**8. A Smith’s Eye: your first upgrade (4 min)**
- In Glacierhome, find **Coldsmith Ingrid Hoarfell** at **Hoarfell’s Cold Forge** (the trader and smith show as gold
  coins on the minimap). Press **F** to talk.
- Choose **“Make something of mine stronger.”** This starts the quest **A Smith’s Eye**.
- In her shop, buy one **Iron Ingot**. Then open the **Runeforge** tab, choose **Upgrade**, pick a piece you like and
  upgrade it to +1. It costs some Embershards and one Iron Ingot.
- **You should see:** the piece now shows **+1**, and A Smith’s Eye completes with a reward of two Iron Ingots.

**9. The other Runeforge tabs (1 min, just look)**
- Still at the Runeforge, look through the tabs: **Reforge**, **Sockets**, **Upgrade**, **Reroll**, **Imbue**,
  **Transfer**, **Craft**, **Salvage**.
- **You should see:** each tab explains itself. **Transfer** moves a unique’s effect from one piece to another (you’ll
  need a unique first), and **Craft** makes runes.

**10. Tomes (later, just note it)**
- A **Tome** teaches one spell from another school. Tomes come from ruin and cave chests and from late bosses, so a
  new hero won’t have one in 20 minutes. If you have an older hero who has found one, use it from the Pack.
- **You should see:** the spell appears in Spells (**B**). You can keep one tome spell at a time.

**11. Settings and the keyboard (2 min)**
- Press **Esc**, choose **Settings**, then the **Accessibility** tab.
- Turn on **Reduce motion**, and try **Text size: Large**.
- Try the menus with the keyboard alone: the arrows or Tab move a gold ring, Enter presses, Esc goes back. On the
  title, Tab reaches Credits, Friends and your account.
- **You should see:** with Reduce motion on, no screen shake, bobbing, pulsing or flicker. Large text stays inside its
  panels.

**12. Co-op with a second window (3 min)**
- Stand at a waystone or in a village. Press **Esc** and choose **Invite friends**.
- **You should see:** a short game code.
- Open a **private (incognito) window** on medivlo.web.app, make a quick hero there, then **Esc › Quit to title** and
  choose **Join Game**. Type the code.
- **You should see:** the second hero appears beside yours. Both can move and fight, chat works with **Enter**, and
  drops are shared. Leave with **Esc › Leave party**.
