# What’s new tonight

Test build for medivlo. Everything below is in this build. Default keys are shown; your rebinds apply in game.

## Deeper builds

- **A new skill tree.** Skills (K) now has three branches per class: Spells, Skills and Upgrades. Perks have ranks, and spending points shows what the next rank does.
- **Keystones.** Rare nodes in the tree that change the rules of how you fight, usually at a price. Your class is offered three, and you can hold two.
- **Weapon mastery.** Each class fights a little differently with each weapon it can use, while that weapon is in your main hand.
- **Rarity and bonus lines.** Gear now rolls a rarity, from Common to Sparkforged. Fine and better gear comes with bonus lines. The words around an item’s name, like “Lamp-lit” or “of Vigour”, come from those lines.
- **Sockets and gems.** Better gear has empty sockets. A smith sets gems in them.
- **Uniques.** 40 of them. The realm bosses and the Sentinels drop legendaries with effects of their own, and the school caves and the Heart hide the rest.
- **Hybrid sets.** Wear two pieces each of two schools’ sets for a two-school bonus. Twelve pairings have their own named bonus, such as Scald, Hailstorm or Dirty Fencing.
- **Runes.** Set them in a spell’s rune slots to change how the spell behaves. Slots open as you learn more of that spell’s path. Take runes out any time.
- **Tomes.** Read one to learn a spell from another school. You keep one at a time.
- **The Runeforge.** At every capital’s smith: Upgrade (up to +5), Reforge to your level, Reroll a bonus line, Imbue a weapon with a school, move a unique’s effect onto another piece, set gems, craft runes and salvage. Walk up to the smith first.
- **Free reset.** Characters made before the new tree get one free reset of every branch.
- **Learning it all.** The first time you meet each system, a one-line hint appears and its Codex (L) page opens. Each capital’s smith also offers a short quest, “A Smith’s Eye”, to walk you through your first upgrade.

## New spells

- 72 new class spells, three for every class, and the 36 school spells can now be learned from tomes.
- Four weak early spells are stronger: Cold Snap, Dancing Steel, Sky Hunt and Net Shot.

## The map and markers

- Quest markers point at the exact person, object or door, not the middle of a zone. A lair boss’s marker points at its lair.
- The tracked quest’s mark shows the place’s name on the map.
- Map labels no longer overlap, and places are named the way the story names them.
- Merchants show as coins on the minimap. Village close-ups show the smithy and the trader.
- Things you’ve already used say “Already searched” or “Already read”. A search that needs several finds counts each object once.
- Tall trees and roofs fade when you walk behind them.

## Co-op and saves

- Playing together is quicker: a party now clears fights faster than one hero alone.
- Bosses keep their eyes on whoever is standing in their face, and tanks hold their attention more firmly, so a party can choose who takes the hits.
- Getting hit while you revive a friend pauses the revive instead of starting it over.
- Rejoining a friend’s game works: a rejoin replaces the old connection.
- Co-op keeps running while the host’s tab is in the background.
- Guests see forged items, runes and tome spells correctly.
- The newest save always wins, whatever the device clocks say. A damaged save value falls back to its default, so the save no longer breaks.
- Public realms use your saved hero.
- Reloading in the middle of an escort or a story scene no longer leaves the quest stuck.
- Esc in a conversation skips the text but keeps what your choices did.

## The text

- Every perk, spell, keystone, rune, unique and set has a proper name and a line of flavour.
- Tooltips use real stat names, give distances in paces, and say “for 4 s” or “once every 6 s” instead of bare brackets.
- The Journal (J) shows each quest’s current step in prose, with a “So far” diary of the earlier steps underneath. The detail scrolls.
- Every key hint follows your rebinds. Settings uses the same names as the tabs, and the potions are called what the items are: draughts and tonics.
- Every class’s first fifteen minutes have been re-read. People are introduced before you’re sent to find them, and objectives say where to go.

## Balance

- The five slowest boss fights are shorter: those bosses have less health.
- Buffs that raise max health, Ember or stamina now actually raise them.
- A shield that soaks a whole hit no longer shows damage.
- Many weak perk and keystone choices got real effects, so more builds work.
- Trinkets now drop, and drops and chests have level floors, so your gear keeps pace with the road.
- Shops and Runeforges need you standing beside the merchant.

## Things to try first

- **Skills (K):** look over the three branches, spend your first points, and find the keystone nodes.
- **Spells (B):** spell cards now show rune slots. The first rune you pick up explains itself.
- **Your first loot:** hover it. The name’s colour is its rarity, its words are its bonus lines, and empty sockets are listed.
- **The smith:** in a capital class’s home city, talk to the smith, choose “Make something of mine stronger.”, then try the Runeforge tabs. Frontier classes meet a smith when they first reach their capital.
- **Journal (J), then Map (M):** track a quest, then open the map. The marker sits on the exact person or door, with the place’s name beside it.
