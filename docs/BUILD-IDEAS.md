# Build ideas: two ways to play every class

Each class has many workable builds. These are two of the strongest, picked to feel different from each other.
Every pick in them held up in testing at level 40, against the class's final boss and its toughest packs.

**How a build fits together**
- **Two keystones:** one from your school (Skills branch, top) and one shared keystone (Upgrades branch). You can
  hold at most two shared keystones.
- **Armour:**
  - your school's full set (4 pieces) for its full bonus;
  - or a **hybrid set**: 2 pieces of your school plus 2 of a neighbouring school, for a combined bonus with its own
    name.
- **A tome** (optional): teaches one spell from another school. Tomes turn up in ruin and cave chests and from late
  bosses.

**When a build comes online**
- The two keystones need 6 to 11 skill points, so every build's core works by **level 12** at the latest.
- The full version, with a hybrid set or a tome, usually comes together in Act II, around **level 18**.

## Rime

**Warden**
- **Shatter Wall:** Glacial Heart, Conduit, your Rime armour, Tome of Meteor. Chill everything that comes close,
  shatter what freezes, and drop a Meteor on the crowd while your aura burns anyone in reach. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **The Patient Winter:** Winter's Patience, Conduit, your Rime armour, Tome of Flame Wall. You move slower but your
  spells hit far harder. Plant yourself behind a wall of fire and let them walk into it. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Rimecaller**
- **Steam Engine:** Winter's Patience, Chain Reaction, the Scald hybrid (Rime + Ash). Heavy-hitting ice spells,
  critical hits that burst into scalding steam, and every kill sets off the next one. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Hailstorm Shatterer:** Glacial Heart, Chain Reaction, the Hailstorm hybrid (Rime + Storm), Tome of Ball
  Lightning. Lightning jumps further and chills as it goes; frozen foes shatter and chain into their neighbours.
  *Level 12, full at 18.*

## Verdant

**Ranger**
- **Briar Sniper:** Thornskin, Burning Bright, the Wildwood hybrid (Verdant + Hunt). Huge damage at range, and
  anything that rushes you gets rooted in thorns while you keep shooting. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Storm Arrows:** Thornskin, The Last Beat, the Pastoral hybrid (Verdant + Song). Every fifth arrow chains
  lightning through the pack, with steady healing to keep you in the fight. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Hedgewitch**
- **Thornheart Healer:** Thornskin, Lifebinder, the Wildwood hybrid, Tome of Piercing Shot. Big heals, rooted
  attackers, and a shot that skewers a whole line. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Ever-Bloom:** Evergreen, Ember Shield, your Verdant armour. Heals that last twice as long and a mana shield that
  soaks hits. You simply refuse to go down. *Level 12.*

## Ash

**Reaver**
- **Executioner's Pyre:** Pyromaniac, Executioner, the Scald hybrid (Ash + Rime), Tome of Blizzard. Burn them down,
  then finish anything under a third of its health with brutal blows. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Blood and Cinders:** Pyromaniac, Bloodkin, your Ash armour, Tome of Ice Lance. Every hit heals you as the burns
  spread. A brawler who never needs to back off. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Arcanist**
- **Burning Bright:** Pyromaniac, Burning Bright, the Scald hybrid, Tome of Blizzard. Glass-cannon fire magic:
  enormous damage, a fragile caster, and a Blizzard to keep them away. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Wildfire:** Controlled Burn, Burning Bright, your Ash armour. Weaker burns, but they leap from foe to foe until
  the whole pack is alight. *Level 12.*

## Storm

**Stormlancer**
- **Live Wire Lancer:** Live Wire, Arcane Edge, the Hailstorm hybrid (Storm + Rime), Tome of Earthshaker. Every
  critical chains lightning, every hit refills your Ember, and Earthshaker floors the crowd. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Spellblade:** Eye of the Storm, Spellblade, the Lightning Rod hybrid (Storm + Iron). Every fourth swing casts
  your first spell for free; block a hit and lightning answers. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Sparkwright**
- **Workshop Pack:** Live Wire, Pack Leader, your Storm armour. Your totems and contraptions hit far harder, and your
  criticals chain lightning between them. *Level 12.*
- **Iron-Fisted Engineer:** Live Wire, Iron Grip, the Lightning Rod hybrid, Tome of Ice Lance. Your spell power
  powers your wrench too. Hit things yourself between casts. *Level 12, full at 18.*

## Radiance

**Oathbound**
- **Burning Oath:** Martyr, Conduit, the Sunfire hybrid (Radiance + Ash), Tome of Meteor. Every heal scorches the foes
  around you, and your aura makes the whole party hit harder. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Haloed Blade:** Halo, Spellblade, the Hymnal hybrid (Radiance + Song). A ring of light burns around you, and every
  fourth swing casts a spell. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Cantor**
- **Choir of Light:** Halo, Conduit, the Hymnal hybrid, Tome of Meteor. Stronger, longer buffs for the party, and an
  aura that strikes foes on its own. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Searing Mercy:** Martyr, Conduit, the Sunfire hybrid. Every heal you cast burns the enemies nearby. Healing *is*
  your damage. *Level 12, full at 18.*

## Umbral

**Shade**
- **Shadowplay:** Shadowborn, Burning Bright, the Shadowplay hybrid (Umbral + Guile). Every kill slips you back into
  shadow, and your first hit from stealth deals triple damage. Chain them through a pack. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Blood Pact:** Blood Pact, Executioner, your Umbral armour, Tome of Piercing Shot. Spells cost health, kills burst
  in a blood nova, and anything left weak is executed. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Duskweaver**
- **Night Stalker:** Shadowborn, Chain Reaction, the Night Stalker hybrid (Umbral + Hunt), Tome of Backstab. Mark a
  foe, strike from the dark, and let each kill explode into the next. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Smoke and Mirrors:** Shadowborn, Chain Reaction, the Shadowplay hybrid, Tome of Smoke Bomb. Vanish, confuse the
  pack, and reappear with a triple-damage hit. *Level 12, full at 18.*

## Blade

**Swordsman**
- **Perfect Duelist:** Riposte Master, Arcane Edge, your Blade armour, Tome of Dragon Kick. Parry, get your dash back,
  strike harder for a moment, and every hit refuels your spells. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Untouchable:** Riposte Master, Untouchable, the Dirty Fencing hybrid (Blade + Guile). Dash constantly; every
  landed dash softens incoming blows and sets up two guaranteed critical hits. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Samurai**
- **Dirty Fencing:** Riposte Master, Burning Bright, the Dirty Fencing hybrid. Parry-heavy precision with huge damage
  and dash-fuelled criticals. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Last Stand:** Berserker Edge, Burning Bright, the Flurry hybrid (Blade + Fist), Tome of Dragon Kick. The more
  you're hurt, the harder you hit, with a flurry of guaranteed criticals. *Level 12, full at 18.*

## Iron

**Knight**
- **Thunder Knight:** Juggernaut, Unmoved, the Lightning Rod hybrid (Iron + Storm), Tome of Thunderclap. Nothing
  knocks you down. Block, and lightning answers; then stun the crowd with thunder. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Living Fortress:** Living Fortress, Fortress, the Iron Body hybrid (Iron + Fist). Your armour adds to your damage,
  you get tougher the longer you stand your ground, and a high combo hardens you further. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Juggernaut**
- **Rolling Thunder:** Living Fortress, Chain Reaction, the Lightning Rod hybrid, Tome of Dragon Kick. Heavy armour,
  heavy hits, and every kill explodes. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Iron Body:** Juggernaut, Chain Reaction, the Iron Body hybrid (Iron + Fist). Unstoppable, extra Momentum, and less
  damage taken while your combo is high. *Level 12, full at 18.*

## Fist

**Monk**
- **Perfect Form:** Perfect Form, Phase Step, your Fist armour. At full Momentum every hit is a critical; dash
  through attacks behind a shield. *Level 12.*
- **Pressure Points:** Pressure Points, Phase Step, the Iron Body hybrid (Fist + Iron), Tome of Blade Dance. Faster,
  harder hits that stun every fifth blow, with tougher skin at high Momentum. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Brawler**
- **Unmoved:** Perfect Form, Unmoved, the Iron Body hybrid, Tome of Blade Dance. Can't be stunned, critical at full
  Momentum, and a spinning flurry of cuts. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Bloodkin Bruiser:** Perfect Form, Bloodkin, the Flurry hybrid (Fist + Blade). Every hit heals you and every
  fourth sets up a critical. Stay in their face. *Level 12, full at 18.*

## Hunt

**Huntsman**
- **Pack Leader:** Lone Wolf, Pack Leader, your Hunt armour. Your traps hit far harder, and you hit harder when no
  ally is close. A lone trapper's paradise. *Level 12.*
- **Night Stalker:** Lone Wolf, Pack Leader, the Night Stalker hybrid (Hunt + Umbral). Every fifth shot marks the foe
  for extra damage from you and your traps. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Falconer**
- **Lone Falcon:** Lone Wolf, Phase Step, your Hunt armour. You and your falcon hunt alone, and dashing out of trouble
  shields you. *Level 12.*
- **Soul-Siphon Falconer:** Lone Wolf, Hoarder, your Hunt armour, Tome of Soul Siphon. Drain health from a whole cone
  of foes while your falcon works, and come home with richer loot. *Level 12, full at 18.*

## Guile

**Corsair**
- **Storm Sailor:** Misdirection, The Last Beat, the Shadowplay hybrid (Guile + Umbral), Tome of Blade Dance. Leave
  decoys behind, chain lightning every fifth hit, and vanish after kills. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Lucky Brawler:** Fortune's Favour, Berserker, the Dirty Fencing hybrid (Guile + Blade). Criticals rain
  Embershards, and you hit harder the closer you are to losing. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Jester**
- **Chain of Laughs:** Fortune's Favour, Chain Reaction, the Dirty Fencing hybrid, Tome of Perfect Parry. Criticals
  everywhere, kills that explode, and a parry that counters for triple damage. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **The Decoy:** Misdirection, Chain Reaction, the Shadowplay hybrid, Tome of Shadow Step. Dodge to leave a decoy,
  teleport through shadow, and let the explosions do the rest. *Level 12, full at 18.*

## Song

**Bard**
- **Crescendo:** Crescendo, Conduit, the Pastoral hybrid (Song + Verdant), Tome of Thorn Volley. Buffs that stack
  three times and an aura that hits on its own, while the party heals steadily. *Level 12, full at 18.*
- **Encore:** Encore Performer, Conduit, the Hymnal hybrid (Song + Radiance), Tome of Sunbeam. Your last buff casts
  itself again for free, and a beam of light burns foes while it heals friends. *Level 12, full at 18.*

**Skald**
- **Battle Saga:** Crescendo, Chain Reaction, your Song armour. Stacked war songs and a battlefield of chain
  explosions. *Level 12.*
- **Encore Saga:** Encore Performer, Chain Reaction, your Song armour, Tome of Sanctuary. Free repeats of your
  strongest song, and a circle where your allies take far less damage. *Level 12, full at 18.*
