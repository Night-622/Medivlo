# 01: The world, the Concord and the Hollow King

## The First Chord (creation, as the schools tell it)
In the beginning the world was **one song**: the First Chord. Everything that lived sang a little of it. When the world grew too large for one song, the Chord broke into **twelve notes**. Every note was a way of being strong:
- **Six were magics:** Ash (fire and change), Rime (cold and stillness), Storm (lightning and speed), Radiance (light and oath), Umbral (shadow and memory), Verdant (growth and rot).
- **Six were crafts of the body:** Blade (the edge), Iron (the wall), Fist (the self), Hunt (the chase), Guile (the angle), Song (the voice).

Those who heard a note most clearly gathered around it and became a **school**. Each school's land slowly took on the colour of its note, and that is how the twelve **realms** came to be. Only at the centre, where all twelve notes could still be heard faintly together, did the land stay ordinary and green. That place is **Concordia**.

*Players should feel this is true in the way myths are true. Characters disagree about the details, and some, the Duskweavers for example, say the Chord didn't break: it was broken.*

## The Concord (C. 0)
A thousand years ago the twelve schools were at war, each sure its note was the true one. The war ended at Concordia, when twelve champions met in a ruined chapel on a hill and agreed the **Concord**:
- Each school gave one **Concord Stone**: a crystal of its note, cut from the heart of its realm.
- The twelve stones were set in **the Concord Crown**, kept in the **Concord Citadel** at the **Heart of Morvane**.
- The first Concordant was crowned in C. 4, when the Citadel was finished. Every twelve years after that, the crown passes to a champion of the next school in the ring: **the Turning**. The wearer is **the Concordant**, monarch of Concordia and keeper of the peace between realms.
- The **Turning Bell** of the hill chapel, which later became the Abbey, rings once at every Turning. It is heard in all twelve realms.
- While the stones sing together in the crown, the **realm-gates** stand open, and anyone may walk from Frostmere to Tidecourt by way of Concordia in a week.

The Concord held for a thousand years and eighty-four Turnings. It had its wars and its scandals, a Tidecourt Concordant who sold the crown jewels twice for instance, but it held.

## The Unsung prince
**Casimir of Concordia** was born in C. 984 to a Radiance Cantor and a Song Bard. He was **Unsung**: born with no school's spark at all. In Concordia that is rare, and pitied. He was raised in the Citadel, tutored by masters of every school, and mastered none. He was clever, patient and kind to servants, and he was hollow in a way no teacher could fill.

At the 85th Turning (C. 1012) the crown should have passed from **Iron** to **Fist**. The Iron Concordant, old Queen Berenika of Anvilgate, had named Casimir her steward, and on the night of the Turning he did not hand the crown on. He put it on.

## The Hollowing (C. 1012)
The stones would not sing for him. An Unsung head cannot hear a note. So Casimir **pried the twelve stones out one by one**, meaning to make them obey him in his hands. Three things happened:
1. **The stones fled.** Each one flew home to its own realm like a struck bird, and **cracked in two** as it fell. Each school now has two **half-stones**, lost in two different corners of its realm. A half-stone will not leave its resting place, or sing, for anyone but its bearer.
2. **The crown wept.** The empty settings began to run with **black tears**: grief made solid, the sorrow of a broken Chord. Whatever the tears touch is **hollowed**: emptied, docile, drawn towards whoever wears the crown. Casimir wore it. He became **the Hollow King**.
3. **The Bell drowned.** The Turning Bell began to ring on its own, endlessly. The Abbey's lake rose over it in one night to silence it. That is the **Drowned Abbey**, and Brother Aldous was the only novice to swim out.

With the stones gone the **realm-gates shut**, and for thirty years the realms have been strangers to one another and to Concordia. Some have prospered. Most have curdled.

## The black tears
- The tears pool at the **Heart of Morvane** and seep outward through Concordia. In Morvane, wolves walk into firelight instead of away from it, iron "rings wrong", graves stand empty and the White Stag weeps black.
- Since last winter **the tears have started seeping through the shut realm-gates**. In every realm the borders sicken first. Creatures "come back wrong", and the local powers have begun to want the half-stones, because a half-stone stops the tears where it lies.
- The tears are **grief, not malice**. A hollowed creature is not evil. It is empty, and it goes looking for something to fill it. That is why the Hollow King's armies are made of the lonely. (Mechanically: Hollowed enemies, black-tear variants.)
- **Hollow iron** is metal touched by the tears. It "remembers being something else", as Odo Vance says.

## Why the bell rang (the inciting incident)
On the first night of the game (**C. 1042**, six years before any Turning was due: the Bell rang out of turn), the Drowned Bell rang once under the lake, as it does at a Turning. It was heard in all twelve realms. In each realm two people woke with **a half-stone's song in their chest**: one in their school's capital, the other on its frontier. Each could hear, faintly, where their half lay hidden. These are the twenty-four heroes, the **bearers**. Each claims their half-stone at the end of their realm's trouble (Act I, lair 3) and carries it through the realm gate to Concordia.

No one knows who rang it. Brother Aldous believes it was the Abbey's drowned choir. Ysolde Fenn believes the crown itself is calling the stones home. The Hollow King knows, and he is waiting.

## The Hollow King's truth (revealed in Act III, varied per class)
- Casimir did not want power. He wanted to **hear the Chord**, just once. When he finally held the crown and heard nothing, he broke it.
- The black tears are **his**. He has wept them for thirty years and cannot stop.
- He needs the twelve stones back in the crown so it can sing again, and he believes that if all twelve sing **inside him**, he will finally be full. Every hero who comes to Concordia carrying a half-stone is bringing him what he wants.
- The heroes' choices at the end decide what the crown becomes: see 05-campaign-framework.md, Endings.
