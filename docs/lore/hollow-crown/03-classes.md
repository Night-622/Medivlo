# 03: The twenty-four classes

Each school has two classes. In every school:
- The **capital class** begins in the school's capital, inside its institutions.
- The **frontier class** begins on the realm's frontier, outside the institutions.

They hold two halves of the same Concord Stone, and each hears the other's half singing. In each campaign the other class is an NPC bearer, first met at the Stone Court in Act II (08 R10). In co-op the two players can simply be both. **Each class has its own campaign** (05-campaign-framework.md) and its own outline (06-outlines/).

What every class carries:
- **Theme:** the question its campaign asks.
- **Mentor:** the NPC who sends them out. Trust with the mentor gates the class's best ending.
- **Nemesis:** a class-unique antagonist met in Act I (lair 3), haunting Act II, and fought finally in Act III, remade by the Hollow King:
  - **magic-school** nemeses become his **Herald** (robe, crown, tears, bell);
  - **martial-school** nemeses are bound into **the Union Chimera**.
  Each nemesis wants the hero's half-stone for a reason that is *almost* right.
- **Morvane bosses (Act II):** A is the school's affinity boss, shared by both classes of the school. B is the class's own pick.

## Magic schools

### Ash (Cinderreach)
| | **Arcanist** (capital: Kilnhold) | **Reaver** (frontier: the Bleeding Fields) |
|---|---|---|
| Look | bent hat with lantern | greataxe over the head |
| Tradition | scholar-mages of the Kiln Conclave, who fire spells like pottery | axe-warriors of the Red Muster, who hold the lava fronts with fire in the blood |
| Theme | *What is fire for?* Burning to destroy, or to change | *Can a war end?* |
| Mentor | **Magister Coalby Thrane**, a soot-faced, absent-minded Conclave master who keeps a pet Lava Slime called Pudding | **Brenna Scald-Hand**, the old war-chief of the Muster, one arm burned black to the elbow |
| Nemesis | **Provost Ignace Morrow**, the Conclave's provost, who has fed the Kiln forbidden things and wants the half-stone to make himself a living kiln that will never go out. Act III: Herald | **Hask the Unburnt**, Brenna's son and the hero's shield-sibling, who went hollow on the Fields and leads the hollow Reavers. He wants a war that never has to end. Act III: Herald |
| Half-stone | the Kiln half | the Field half |
| Morvane A / B | Ashmaw / the Gilded Engine | Ashmaw / Bonelord Varric |

## Coverage check (Morvane boss as B)
- Gilded Engine: Arcanist, Sparkwright, Corsair
- Bonelord Varric: Reaver, Oathbound
- Lantern Queen: Rimecaller, Duskweaver
- White Stag: Warden, Ranger, Skald
- Rimeheart Colossus: Stormlancer, Juggernaut
- Weeping Willow: Cantor, Shade, Bard
- Ooze Sovereign: Hedgewitch
- Stormcrow: Swordsman, Monk, Falconer
- Spinner Below: Samurai, Huntsman
- Ashmaw: Knight, Brawler
- Abbess Morwen: Jester

Every Morvane boss is someone's A, so every boss is on at least two story paths. The rest are optional in every campaign (as bounties: see 05).
