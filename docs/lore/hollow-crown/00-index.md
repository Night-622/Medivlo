# The Hollow Crown: lore bible (index)

Owner: **Claude 4** (narrative). This replaces the retired "Medival" bible in `docs/lore/*.md`. Old ideas are reused only where they fit, and **the handoff's names win** (`Medieval Browser Action RPG/`).

## The pitch
The world is a ring of **twelve realms**, one for each school of craft or magic, around **Concordia**, the union at its centre. For a thousand years the schools kept the **Concord**: a crown set with **twelve Concord Stones**, one from each school, worn in turn by a champion of each school. While the stones sang together, the realm-gates stood open and the schools lived as one.

Thirty years ago a man of Concordia, born with no school's spark, took the crown out of turn. The stones would not sing for him, so he pried them out. The empty crown began to weep **black tears**, and he became **the Hollow King**. The twelve stones fled home to their realms, cracking in two as they fell. The realm-gates shut.

Now the tears are seeping through the gates. In every realm creatures "come back wrong", people go Hollow, and the old powers fight over the half-stones. Last night, in every realm at once, a drowned bell rang. Each of the twenty-four heroes hears it, hears their half of their school's stone singing somewhere in their realm, and sets out to find it. The finding is the realm's story; the road after it leads to Concordia and the Hollow King.

## Canon rules
- **Names:** the handoff's names win (realms, capitals, landmarks, classes, NPCs, creatures, bosses, regions, villages). New names go in the canon log below.
- **Calendar:** years are counted from the founding of the Concord ("of the Concord", **C.**). The Hollow came in **C. 1012**; the game begins in **C. 1042**, "the thirtieth year of the Hollow".
- **Currency:** Embershards. **Mana:** Ember.
- **Tone:** bright, warm, chibi-pixel on the surface, with real grief underneath. Every realm is funny before it is sad. No grimdark: the black tears are sorrow made solid, not gore.
- **Dialogue voice:** short lines (handoff style, about 1–3 sentences). Characters are distinct and specific. British spelling, curly apostrophes, no modern idiom.

## Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Concord** | the thousand-year pact of the twelve schools |
| **Concordia** | the union at the centre of the ring; contains the land of **Morvane** |
| **The Concord Crown** | the crown of the Concord, now empty: the Hollow Crown |
| **Concord Stone** | one of twelve stones, one per school. Each split into two **half-stones** when it fell |
| **The Turning** | the passing of the crown every twelve years to the next school's champion, the **Concordant** |
| **The Hollow King** | Casimir of Concordia, who emptied the crown in C. 1012 |
| **Black tears** | what the empty crown weeps; it hollows whatever it touches |
| **Hollowed** | a creature or person emptied by the tears and drawn towards the King |
| **Realm gate** | each realm's door to Concordia, shut since the Hollow |
| **The Drowned Bell** | the Turning Bell of the Abbey, drowned in C. 1012. It rang again on the night the game begins |
| **Sentinel** | one of the eleven Sentinels of the Turning Road, the hollowed Morvane bosses (one per school; Morwen serves two) |
| **Unsung** | a person born with no school's spark (rare; Casimir was one) |
