# 04: Peoples, Cultures, Languages, Glossary

## Peoples

These could be ancestry options in character creation (Claude 1 / Claude 3 decide). Each has a naming kit so every agent can generate consistent names.

### Haldish (humans of the Haldmarch)
- **Look:** fair to ruddy, brown and red hair; wool, leather, undyed linen; the rich wear blue and ochre.
- **Culture:** farmers, shepherds and yeomen; stubborn, practical, superstitious. Old Hearth faith with a Lanternate veneer. Proud of the Charter. Distrust both Solenne and magic.
- **Weapons:** longbow, bill, arming sword, longsword, kite shield.
- **Names (Old English flavour):** *Aldric, Godric, Osric, Edwy, Wat, Tobin, Garrick, Hild, Wenna, Maren, Aldith, Edith, Merewyn, Cuthbert, Oswin, Elfreda*. Surnames from trades and places: *Hollis, Thatch, Coalbrook, Fennick, Ashby, Harrow, Pryde, Barrowborn*.
- **Place-name parts:** *-moor, -mere, -wick, -ford, -cross, -ley, -den, -field, -stow, Brindle-, Wenna-, Alder-*.
- **Speech:** plain, earthy, with proverbs ("Cold hearth, cold heart."). Oaths: "Seven keep us," "Hearth and kin."

### Solenni (imperial humans of the south)
- **Look:** olive to dark skin, black hair; white and gold silks, enamel, perfumed oil.
- **Culture:** urban, literate, ceremonious; the Lanternate is their identity. They see the Haldish as rustic cousins and everyone else as heathen. The snow now falling on their vineyards is a theological terror.
- **Weapons:** rapier, mace and flail (clergy "shed no blood"), crossbow, tower shield.
- **Names (Latin/Occitan flavour):** *Aurelian, Castor, Vellane, Seraphine, Ysolde, Lucan, Ottavia, Caius, Ilaria, Benedek, Sabine*. Houses: *de Vaumont, Castellane, Aurevine*.
- **Place-name parts:** *Sol-, Val-, -enne, -castre, -ac, Aure-*.
- **Liturgical phrases (pseudo-Latin "High Solenni"):** *"Lux manet."* (The light remains.) *"Fiat flamma."* *"Ignis Ithreni, tenebra cedit."* (By Ithren's fire, darkness yields.)

### Hrothi (northern humans)
- **Look:** pale and tall, blond to black hair, braids, tattoos of wolves and storms; furs, mail.
- **Culture:** clan-moots, sagas, sea-raiding. They worship Calun (storms) and Vor (endurance) most. Oath-honour above law.
- **Weapons:** bearded axe, round shield, seax, spear.
- **Names (Norse flavour):** *Sigrun, Hrolf, Thrain, Ingrid, Bjorna, Ulfar, Eydis, Ketil, Ragna*. Bynames: *Ice-Eye, Wolfsbane, the Unbowed*.
- **Place-name parts:** *-vik, -heim, -gard, -fjord, Skel-, Hrim-*.

### Aelthi (the forest folk)
- **Look:** tall and slender, long-lived (400+ years), eyes with a faint inner light; skin from birch-pale to bark-brown, hair in leaf and silver tones. Wardwood ornaments that keep growing.
- **Culture:** song-based magic and memory, and deep grief over the Sundering of Ys. Insular, polite, melancholy. They see humans as quick, bright and dangerous, like sparks. Most are Thornwise.
- **Weapons:** glaive, thornbow (living wardwood), leaf-blade knives.
- **Names (Welsh flavour):** *Rhiannon, Cadoc, Gwyneira, Taliesyn, Ynys, Bryn, Eluned, Gwyl, Aeronwy, Cerys*. Lineage: *ap* (son of) / *ferch* (daughter of), *Ys-Caer* (of the drowned court).
- **Words:** *aer* song · *ylwen* white grove · *caer* hold, fort · *ys* below, drowned · *hun* sleep · *Ae'Hun* the Dreaming (the Mire) · *gwyn* bright · *hiraeth-ys* grief for the drowned.

### Orrun (the stone-folk)
- **Look:** short, broad, very heavy; grey-brown skin that looks like weathered stone in old age; eyes like banked coals. Beards and hair are braided with iron rings.
- **Culture:** descendants of the Orrin giants. Hold-clans, oath-ledgers, ancestor-forges. They revere Vor and Orr. Slow to trust; a debt is never forgotten.
- **Weapons:** warhammer, pick, runeforged greataxe, heavy plate.
- **Names (hard consonants):** *Brannoch, Dhurm, Korrag, Ulka, Grimma, Thrask, Hedda, Orrik, Baldur*. Clan names: *Deepforge, Ironsong, Coalheart, Stonebrow*.
- **Words:** *dun* hall · *korr* iron · *orr* father, maker · *nagh* oath · *grenn* rust (a curse word) · *"Korr nagh."* (Iron-oath: an unbreakable promise.)

### Qasiri (the desert people)
- **Look:** brown to dark skin, often with ritual ash-scarring; layered sand-coloured robes, glass and copper jewellery.
- **Culture:** nomad caravans, oracle-matriarchs, hospitality laws ("salt shared is a vow"). They carry the shame of Qadir and are deeply cautious about magic, yet skilled at it. They follow a mild Ashvar faith.
- **Weapons:** nar-sabre (curved), cinderglass dagger, throwing rings.
- **Names (Arabic/Persian flavour):** *Ruqaya, Samir, Zahra, Idris, Laleh, Tariq, Nasrin, Ozrah* (an ill-omened name). Lineage: *al-* / *bint* / *ibn*.
- **Words:** *nar* fire · *zahr* bloom (an oasis) · *qadr* fate (hence Qadir, "the fated city").

### The Drowned (not a playable people)
- Humans and beasts fully taken by the Mire: pale, with black tears, always wet, speaking in the **Drowned Tongue**. That language is long vowels and soft consonants, written as doubled vowels with apostrophes: *"Ooluun ae Nhal, maa'reh suun."* ("Below is Nhal; there, sleep.") Players can partly learn to read it through codex entries.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Morvane** | the continent (and, loosely, the world) |
| **The Mire** | the primordial dreaming sea beneath and before the world; "the Dreaming" (Aelthi), "Below" (cultists) |
| **Orr** | the smith-god who forged the world; dead; his body is the Ironspine |
| **Heartfire** | Orr's still-burning heart at the world's core; source of all ember |
| **Ley** | a vein of the Heartfire's heat running through the world |
| **Ember** | the magical heat mages draw from leys (the mana resource) |
| **The Seven Sparks** | the seven gods: Ithren, Vor, Saela, Kethra, Morrow, Ashvar, Calun |
| **Nhal** | the Eighth Spark, drowned in the Mire |
| **The Brand** | the living coal of the Heartfire kept in Solenne cathedral from 0 YL until the Quench |
| **The Quench / Night of Falling Ash** | Lanternmass 1186 YL: the Brand was put out and shattered |
| **The Seven Embers** | the shards of the Brand, scattered across Morvane; the campaign's MacGuffins |
| **Ashmark** | the player's scar: a splinter of a falling Ember, fused into the hand |
| **Hollowed** | a living thing touched or taken by the Mire |
| **Unwrought** | creatures born directly from the Mire; formless or wrongly shaped |
| **Mire seep** | a place where black Mire-water rises through the ground; it spreads over the acts |
| **Cinderlung** | the cough and burns from overdrawing ember |
| **Wick-licence** | Church permission to practise magic |
| **Lanternmass** | the midwinter festival of light; now also the anniversary of the Quench |
| **Remembrance pyre** | a village bonfire lit for the dead of a disaster |
| **The Long Winter** | the unending winter since the Quench |
| **The Wrought** | Orrin giant constructs; also the First-Age Dominion |
| **The Nine Nails** | true-iron spikes the Orrin drove to pin the Mire; two have failed |
| **The Lullaby (Hun-Aerwen)** | the Aelthi song meant to sing the Mire to sleep; it sank Ys Aerwen |
| **True-iron** | Orrin giant-metal that never rusts |
| **Heartstone** | crystallised Heartfire; mythic |
| **The Restless** | the dead who cannot cross Saela's freezing River Under |
| **River Under** | the river by which Saela carries the dead to Morrow |
| **YL** | Years of the Lantern; 0 YL is the founding of Solenne |

## Class origins (Claude 3's roster)

| Class | In-world origin | Dialogue hooks |
|---|---|---|
| **Warden** | shield-sworn of the Marches; village militia or household knight | Hesta notes the name: "Warden. Hm. We'll see if you earn the rest of it." |
| **Ranger** | Thornwise-taught hunter; knows the Gloamwood | Nan Briar and Maren treat you as kin; you can track Tobin without help |
| **Arcanist** | ley-scholar, a hedge-school student, maybe unlicensed | the Inquisitor demands your Wick-licence; Anselm can forge one |
| **Reaver** | Bloodcraft: draws ember from your own body | the Church calls it a step to Mirecraft; the Choir whispers to you; extra Hollowed dialogue |
| **Shade** | the Quiet Hand (Haldren thieves' guild) or Veil-touched | you can pick the miller's strongbox; you notice the cultist signs first |
| **Oathbound** | Lightsworn of the Lanternate; Inquisitor subclass = Order of the Wick | the Inquisitor treats you as a colleague; a crisis of faith when you learn the truth |

All classes are **Ashmarked**. The mark is the story's spine; the class is how you survive it.
