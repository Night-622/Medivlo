# 09: Regions: History and Lore

Canonical list agreed with Claude 2 (see `docs/world/WORLD.md` for layout, tiers and biomes). Rough layout: Aldermoor south-centre; Kingsvale north of it; Witherdowns west; Greywater (Varn) east; Lantern Coast and Sunscar south; Mirelands east of Greywater; Cinder Wastes south-east; Ironspine and Foothills across the north-centre with the Frostreach beyond; Ylwen west; Saltreach Coast on the west coast, with the Isles offshore. The Heartforge is an instance beneath the Vault of the Nine Nails.

### Aldermoor
Heather moor and a sheltered valley ringed by tors. In the Second Age an Aelthi holy grove stood here; its **Weeping Stones** (a stone circle that sweats water even in frost) still stand in the **Gloamwood**. The Orrin giants built the **Giant's Sluice** in the First Age to tame Wennamere: nobody knows why giants cared about a mere. (The truth, revealed in Act II: Wennamere was a minor Mire seep, and the sluice is a lesser cousin of the Nine Nails.) Haldish settlers founded **Brindlecross** around 600 YL at the crossroads of the Aelthi paths. The **Abbey of Saint Wenna** (around 780 YL) stood on an islet. On the Night of Falling Ash an Ember fell into the mere, the black water rose, and the abbey sank. **Edric's Barrow**, on the moor, is the tomb of one of King Edric Barrowborn's sworn knights; legend says Edric himself is buried here and not in the Witherdowns.

### Greywater Marsh
Reed-sea and black fens, drained in part by Orrin works in the First Age, and long the place where the Mire presses closest to the surface. **Varn** was chartered in 702 YL as a fen-drainage town, rich on eels, peat and salt. Its lord **Aldwin Varn** lost his family to the Grey Lung and turned to the Choir; on the Night of Falling Ash he opened the flood-gates. Varn is now half-drowned, with bells ringing underwater. Also here: the **Reed Temples** of the Choir, the **Black Seep** (the origin of the Act III Breach), and stilt-villages of eel-fishers caught between cult and crown. Because of the Varn Causeway, the marsh is the Haldmarch's eastern gate.

### Kingsvale
The fertile heart of the Haldmarch along the River Hald. **Haldren**, the capital, grew around **Haldren Keep**, where the **Charter of Haldren** was sealed in 870 YL. Now it's walled, overcrowded with refugees, and ruled by a 15-year-old queen and her regent. On a crag above the vale stand the ruins of **Cinderhold**, seat of the Emberwardens until the Purge of 1041 YL; the blackened walls still feel warm. Restored by the player, it becomes the hub base. Also: farm-villages starving in the Long Winter, the river-port of Haldford, and the hidden dens of the Quiet Hand in Haldren's undercity.

### Witherdowns
Chalk downs studded with the barrows of the **Barrow-Kings**, the pre-Charter Haldish kings. The **Grey Monks of Morrow** keep the **Ossuary of Saint Calder** and its archive of memory, whose library holds the oldest human records of the Mire. Since the Quench, Saela's River Under is freezing, so the dead aren't crossing over: the **Restless** walk the downs at night. The main dungeon is **the King-Barrows**. Edric Barrowborn's empty tomb is here, a mystery that points to Aldermoor.

### Saltreach Coast
A storm-beaten western coast of cliffs and coves. **Brackhaven** has been a free port since the War of the Two Lanterns (960–978 YL), when it refused both Hierophant and King, and it is the seat of the Salt League. Saela's tide-shrines line the cliffs; her priests (Rime mages) still bless ships, though the sea has started to freeze in the harbour at night.

### Ironspine Foothills
Mining towns on the southern slopes of the great range. **Dun Korrath-Gate** is the Orrun trade-gate, the only place most humans ever see an Orrun hold. Frozen Orrin giants stand along the ridges like statues. Ley-veins glow in the rock. Since the Quench, the mines are flooding with something black from below.

### Ylwen Deepwood
An ancient, vast forest of the Aelthi. In the canopy is **Caer Ylwen**, the living enclave, grown rather than built. In the lake below lie the sunken ruins of **Ys Aerwen**, the Second-Age court that sank in the **Sundering of Ys** (around 1100 BL) when the Aelthi sang the Lullaby to the Mire. The **Thornwise Circle** meets in the Grove of Antlers. The forest is closed to outsiders unless the Circle grants a pact.

### Ironspine
The high range that is Orr's body. **Korrath-under-Stone** is the great Orrun hold, carved into the god's ribs. Deeper still is the **Vault of the Nine Nails**, where the Orrin drove true-iron spikes to pin the Mire. Two have failed, and the Concord hides it. Beneath the Vault is the way to the **Heartforge**. Hostile: the Wrought, still patrolling; Rustwraiths in the abandoned deeps.

### Sunscar
Southern dunes and red canyons. The **Qasiri** caravans cross it from oasis to oasis. **Zahrun**, the oasis-city, is home to the Cindermother. Buried Qadiri waystations hold glass tablets with the Ashen Kings' records. This is the only region where it's still warm, and even here the nights now bring frost.

### Lantern Coast
The imperial-holy south: olive terraces, vineyards, white cities, and now **snow**, which has never been seen there. **Solenne**, founded in 0 YL around the Brand, is the seat of the Hierophant. The **Cathedral of the Brand** is now dark, its great lantern a cracked black husk. **Valcastre** is the Order of the Wick's fortress-prison. Pilgrims in their thousands camp outside the walls, praying for the light to return.

### Saltreach Isles
A storm archipelago reached only by ship. It has corsair holds, smugglers' caves, and the **Drowned Tide-Temples** of Saela, older than the Lantern and half-submerged. One of the Embers fell into the sea here and was fished up by the Salt League.

### Frostreach
Tundra, fjords and advancing glaciers north of the Ironspine. **Skelvik** is the port-hall of Jarl Sigrun Ice-Eye. The **Wolf-Moot Stones** are where the clans gather and where an Ember fell. Under the ice lie First-Age Orrin ruins (the "frozen old empire" of Hrothi legend). The Long Winter is killing the north, and the young warriors want to take the south.

### Cinder Wastes
Black ash dunes and glass flats to the south-east, burned by the Ashen Kings (the Burning of Qadir, around 400 BL). The **Ruins of Qadir** are glass palaces half-sunk in ash, where the ghost of **King Ozrah the Unsleeping** still holds court. **Ashwyrms** and feral **cinderdrakes** hunt here. The Ashen Creed's hidden stronghold is in the ruins. The **Ash Road** from Sunscar is the only safe way in. Under Qadir is the Heartforge's "back door".

### Mirelands (the Hollow Sea)
Not a place but an *unplace*: what Greywater Marsh becomes when the Breach opens at the end of Act II. Rain falls upward, memories take physical form, and the dead and the dreaming walk together. It grows with `world.mire_level`.

### Heartforge
The world's core: the forge-chamber where Orr struck the world, and where his heart still burns, dimmer each year. Saint Solenne came here in 0 YL, Ysolde in 1181 YL. The final dungeon.
