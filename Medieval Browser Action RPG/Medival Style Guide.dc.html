04Character proportions
HEAD
21px
BODY
16px
PIVOT · bottom-centre (16, 38)
Frame 32×40 for humanoids. Art stays inside columns 3–30 and rows 1–38 so the auto-outline always fits.
Proportion Head and headgear take about 21px, body and feet about 16px. That puts humanoids at roughly 2.2 heads tall. Hats may add height; the face never shrinks.
Face Two 2×3 eyes with a 1px glint, 2px blush, 2px mouth. Enemies swap the glint for glow colour (Hollowed eyes are #f4a6ff over #b84dff) and carry black tear streaks.
Shading 3 tones per material plus ink. Light from the top-left; the far side and the underside of brims get the dark tone.
Motion Idle: 2 frames, the upper body rises 1px, 2.5 fps. Walk: 4 frames (contact A, pass, contact B, pass), 8 fps. Weapons and shields are separate layers with their own outline, so they swap per class and per direction.
Silhouette Each class owns one shape at 1×: Arcanist the brim hat, Warden the plume and shield. Check new designs as a flat ink silhouette before colouring.

05Tiles
32×32 at 1×. Ground tiles have no outline. Variety comes from mixing 3 plain variants with 1 flower variant in about one tile in six, and from grass tufts that overhang the edge of paths. Props stand on the ground with a soft indigo shadow and a full ink outline. The full Gloamwood and Drowned Abbey sets come with the tile sheet in phase 2; these samples fix the colour of each region.

06Effects
Effects use flat pixel shapes and additive radial glows only, with no blur or bloom. Every school has a mid colour for shapes and a pale colour for cores and sparks. Light is drawn after the night grade, so magic and fire always read brighter than the world around them.
